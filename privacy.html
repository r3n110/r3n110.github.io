Privacy policy

mothership backup is used only by its owner, for the owner's own data.

  • It requests only the Google Drive drive.file scope, so it can see and change only the files it creates. It cannot read other files in the Drive.
  • The files it creates are encrypted backups of the owner's own home server.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account's third-party access settings.
